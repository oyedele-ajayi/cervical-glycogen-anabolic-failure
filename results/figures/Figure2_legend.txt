Figure 2. Expression of the 33 panel genes across disease grade, discovery
cohort (GSE63514, n = 128). Columns are samples ordered by grade and, within
each grade, by ascending GDI_diff. Rows follow the locked module order:
Synthesis (9), Degradation (9), Warburg (8), Differentiation (7).
Values are z-scores computed within each gene across all samples, with the colour scale clipped at +/-2.5, the 98th percentile of |z|;
2.2% of cells exceed this limit and are drawn at the extreme colour.
